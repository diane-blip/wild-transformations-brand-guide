01 — BRAND STORY

Born from a ritual,
built for transformation

Wild Transformations began with a single, body-deep experience. After a complicated birth, you took part in a Closing of the Bones ceremony — a traditional postpartum ritual practiced across many cultures.

Being witnessed by your closest friends helped you process a difficult birth story and reconnect with your own body. That moment revealed what talk alone often can’t reach — and it became the seed of a practice that braids evidence-based therapy with intentional ceremony.

With more than two decades supporting people across hospitals, community mental health, addiction recovery, and LGBTQ+ services, you built Wild Transformations as a space where people can release old stories, reconnect with their values, and step into who they’re becoming.

Mission

Why we exist

To help people navigating grief, identity shifts, and major life transitions release what no longer serves them and reclaim their power — through therapy and ritual that reach below the level of language.

Vision

The world we’re building

A world where meaningful transitions are honored, not rushed — where being witnessed is recognized as medicine, and healing is understood as active transformation rather than something to simply talk through.

02 — NICHE & POSITIONING

Who this is for

You work with adults at a turning point — people who want the past to stay in the past and are ready for real movement.

  • Neurodivergent folks, HSPs, and empaths who feel deeply
  • High achievers who look strong outside but feel stuck or overloaded inside

  • People resolving trauma — and anyone drawn to a more soulful approach to healing

For people standing at a meaningful threshold, Wild Transformations is the practice that pairs evidence-based therapy with intentional ritual — so transformation is felt in the body, not just talked about.

If You’re Here…

Wherever you are in your journey, this is a space for reflection, healing, and transformation.

04 — VOICE & TONE

How the brand sounds

Your voice is warm and grounded, soulful but never woo-heavy. It speaks to intelligent, sensitive people like an equal — honest, witnessing, and quietly poetic.

Grounded

Steady and clear. Earthy imagery — bones, rivers, feathers, stars — kept concrete, never floaty.

Warm & Direct

Compassionate but honest. Names the hard thing kindly. Directness is a form of care.

Soulful, not woo

Holds ritual and science with equal respect. Mystical language always anchored in real outcomes.

Affirming

Inclusive by default. Identity-honoring, plain-spoken, never clinical or cold.

Do & Don’t

✓ Write like this

  • “Release old stories and reclaim your power.”
  • “Some life transitions deserve more than conversation alone.”
  • “Healing that lives below the level of language.”
  • Speak directly to “you.” Use sensory, earthy metaphor.
  • Honor identity explicitly; invite, never pressure.

× Not like this

  • “Unlock your best self with our proven 5-step system!”
  • “Manifest abundance and raise your vibration today.”
  • “Patients present with maladaptive coping mechanisms.”
  • Avoid hype, hustle, exclamation-heavy marketing.
  • Avoid cold clinical jargon — and avoid vague woo.

07 — LOGO USAGE

The mark

A tree whose canopy is formed from water droplets — growth, flow, and renewal. The full lockup is the primary signature.

  • Give the mark clear space of at least the height of the trunk on every side.
  • Minimum width 120 px on screen / 1 inch in print to keep the wordmark legible.
  • The brown wordmark needs a light ground — reverse onto a light card when placing on dark or photographic backgrounds.

Backgrounds — keep the mark legible

Left two: approved. Right two: the droplet canopy survives on dark grounds, but the brown wordmark falls away — only use the mark on dark when contained in a light disc (as on the cover) or paired with reversed type.

Please don’t

Recolor the foliage, stretch or skew the mark, or rebuild it in another typeface.

Add drop shadows, outlines, or gradients, or place the mark on a busy area of a photo.

Pairing rules

Gilda Display

Headlines · titles · pull quotes

Use for display sizes only. Generous size, tight leading, sentence case. Let it breathe — never set long paragraphs in Gilda.

PT Serif

Body · captions · UI

All running text, lists, labels, and buttons. Line length 60–75 characters, line-height ~1.7 for comfortable, unhurried reading.

10 — PHOTOGRAPHY & IMAGERY

Grounded, soulful, real

Two image worlds work together: warm, natural-light portraits of you, and intimate close-ups of ritual objects — stones, cards, feathers, and sacred tools. Cosmic backdrops (night skies, water) tie them to the brand.

✓ Reach for

  • Soft, natural window light and shallow depth of field
  • Real hands, real objects — stones, feathers, cards, textiles
  • Earthy teal & warm neutral tones; the green hair as a signature note
  • Cosmic backdrops — night sky, still water — for atmosphere

× Avoid

  • Generic corporate stock or staged “therapy office” clichés
  • Cold blue-grey filters or heavy saturation
  • Literal new-age tropes (glowing chakras, clip-art mandalas)
  • Busy compositions that crowd out stillness and space
